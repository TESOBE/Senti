TESOBE Senti

Senti watches your APIs, Apps, Services and Pages and suggests security and performance fixes.

Senti reads your source code, your logs your metrics and telemetry - for hours, not seconds - and gives your developers the most impact issues to fix.

What Senti looks at

01

Source code

Reads the code of every endpoint that can be reached, open ones first, looking for SQL injection, missing authorisation checks and similar problems. It reads the exact commit each server is running.

02

Logs

Strips ids, numbers, emails and other details from each line, so one problem is one signature, then counts every signature over time. A thousand timeouts become one finding, not a thousand alerts.

03

Metrics and telemetry

Follows call counts, response times and latencies, and compares the last hours with the hours before. It notices what is getting slower before your users do.

One short digest

Senti's AI analyst joins the dots between the code, the logs and the numbers, then writes down only what is worth a developer's time.

  • Three suggestions at mostAnd no more than ten a week, ranked by impact, confidence, effort and trend
  • No blipsIt waits for enough hours of watching, and ignores what happened only once
  • No repeatsIt never brings back what you fixed, dismissed or parked for later
  • Quiet when all is wellWhen nothing is worth your time, the digest says so
  • It learnsTell it what you accepted, acted on or dismissed, and it adjusts
  • It checksWhen you act on a suggestion, it watches whether the problem went away

Safe by design

  • Read-onlySenti never changes settings, edits code or opens pull requests
  • On your infrastructureIt runs next to your API, with its own read-only credentials
  • Capped AI spendEvery analysis and every code review has its own budget limit
  • Your own AI accountSenti uses Claude through your own account and budget
